Know Thyself — Find Peace Within

Begin a journey inward with Know Thyself, a serene meditation and spiritual
audio app guided by Anurag Rishi. Whether you are seeking calm after a long

WHAT YOU GET

• Guided Meditations — Gentle, spoken sessions to relax, refocus, and reconnect
  with yourself.

• Spiritual Talks & Teachings — Listen to wisdom that brings clarity, purpose,
  and inner peace.

• Beautiful, Calming Design — A soothing dark-violet interface designed to keep
  you relaxed, free of clutter and distraction.

• Offline Listening — Download your favourite sessions and listen anywhere,
  anytime, even without an internet connection.

• Background Playback — Keep listening while you use other apps or with your
  screen off, with handy notification controls.

• Continue Where You Left Off — The app remembers your progress so you can pick
  up your session right where you paused.

• Sleep Timer — Drift off peacefully and let the audio fade away on its own.

• Personal Profile — Track your journey and manage your account with ease.

WHY KNOW THYSELF?

In a noisy, fast-moving world, true peace comes from within. Know Thyself is
your daily companion for mindfulness and self-discovery — a quiet space to
breathe, reflect, and return to your center.

Download Know Thyself today and take the first step toward a calmer, more
mindful you.

— Find Peace Within —
