Delete Your Account

App: Know Thyself

You can request deletion of your Know Thyself account and all associated personal data at any time by contacting us directly.

To request account deletion, email us at:

ar.happinessmovement@gmail.com

Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
Include the email address associated with your account.

What gets deleted

Upon receiving your request, we will delete the following within 30 days:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Your email address
  • Your device registration and fingerprint
  • Your listening history and playback progress
  • Your subscription records
  • Any downloaded content linked to your account

What may be retained

  • Anonymised, aggregated usage statistics (not linked to you)
  • Records required by law or for fraud prevention (if applicable)

Partial data deletion (optional)

If you would like to delete only specific data (e.g. listening history or device records) without deleting your account, you may request this in the same email. Please specify which data you would like removed.

Response time

We will acknowledge your request within 3 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.
