Privacy Policy

App: Know Thyself  ·  Last updated: 21 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Know Thyself ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information. By using the app you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your email address, used to create and sign in to your account.
  • Device information: A device identifier (fingerprint) used to enforce our one-device-per-account policy and protect premium content.
  • Usage data: Playback progress and listening history, so you can resume audio where you left off.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and provide access to your account.
  • To deliver, stream, and let you download meditation and spiritual audio content.
  • To enforce content licensing (one active device per account).
  • To remember your progress and improve your experience.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (authentication and database) and Cloudflare R2 (audio storage). Downloaded audio is stored on your device using AES-256 encryption and is never saved in an unprotected form. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as required to operate the app (our hosting and storage providers) or to comply with the law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing us at the address below.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
